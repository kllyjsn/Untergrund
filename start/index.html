GETTING STARTED

Make your first track.

UNTERGRUND writes and plays original music in your browser. Start with a genre or an idea, then take the track into Studio, share it, or play it live.

Start creating
  1. 01

    Create

    Pick a genre and press Generate, or use Describe it to start with words. Open Refine in Studio to edit the result.

  2. 02

    Listen & remix

    Play a release from the shelf on Create. Open its title for the track page, then choose Remix this track to make your own version.

  3. 03

    Join the Club

    Open Club to see which rooms are live, or follow a room invite. To play your own track for the room, choose Go Live from Now Playing.

  4. 04

    Keep it or release it

    Library keeps tracks in this browser, not across devices. Use Publish in Now Playing for a public track page when publishing is available, or export a WAV from Studio to keep a copy.
