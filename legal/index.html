02

Privacy notice

UNTERGRUND uses first-party product signals and minimal account data. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable first-party analytics and enjoyment signals.

Data we process

  • Device and account data: a signed session cookie, anonymous device account, claimed handle, profile fields, follows and linked SoundCloud account identifiers when you choose to connect them.
  • Music and public activity: projects you publish, musical state, titles, artist names, remixes, public room activity, chat, reactions, recordings you upload and related metadata.
  • Prompts and lyrics: requests sent to Describe or song planning. Requests the local rules cannot handle may be sent to the configured Google AI or OpenRouter model. When analytics is enabled, short Describe misses may be retained to improve command understanding.
  • Product analytics: anonymous app events, errors, random session and device identifiers, genre or feature context, and listening signals including start, completion, skip, replay, keep, publish, share, live and love. Listening events include a song fingerprint, seed, style, source, playback surface and progress. Prompt text is included only on a start event, capped at 120 characters and omitted when it resembles an email, URL or phone number.
  • Technical data: approximate network identifiers used transiently for security, deduplication and rate limiting; browser and route information in error reports; push subscription endpoints when you opt into notifications.
  • Legal requests: your name, email address, request type, relevant work or URL, and the details you submit through this page.

Why we process it

We process data to provide requested features, preserve projects, run public rooms, publish tracks, prevent abuse, secure the service, diagnose failures, measure whether music is enjoyed, improve generation, comply with law and respond to rights or privacy requests. Where law requires a legal basis, those purposes rely on performance of the service agreement, legitimate interests in operating and improving the service, consent for optional features, and legal obligations.

Choices

Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable first-party analytics and listening signals. You can avoid connecting SoundCloud, decline push notifications, keep rooms private and avoid publishing. You may request access, correction, deletion, portability or objection where applicable by using the form below. Some records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance or dispute resolution.

Sharing and processors

Data may be processed by infrastructure and feature providers acting for their own or our purposes, including Vercel for hosting and page analytics, Upstash for data storage, SoundCloud for linked accounts and publishing, browser push services for notifications, and Google AI or OpenRouter for prompts routed to optional model-backed features. Public content is shared with the public by design. We do not sell personal information for money.

Retention and security

Retention depends on the feature. Live rooms expire after seven idle days. Push subscriptions inactive for sixty days are removed during sends. Describe-miss logs are capped and expire after about four hundred days. Legal requests expire after one hundred eighty days unless preservation is reasonably required. Public tracks, account records and provenance records may remain while the feature or account is active or while needed to protect rights and service integrity. No security measure is perfect; use the service accordingly.

International use

UNTERGRUND and its providers may process data in countries other than yours. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. We use the safeguards available through our providers and applicable law.

04

Provenance and licensing

Every source that enters the production engine should be attributable, reproducible and safe for the intended use.

UNTERGRUND's procedural synthesis is generated by code. Bundled recorded instruments are accepted only from approved public-domain, CC0 or commercially safe sources, with source repository, pinned revision and license records. Public-domain repertoire is imported only when the source file's license is verified. The current records are published in the recorded-instrument register and the repertoire register.

For each published song, the intended provenance chain includes the deterministic song fingerprint, generator version, seed, prompt hash, source path, edits, section map, instruments, public samples, collaborators and render version. Future learned performers, including neural singers, must add model identity, training-rights basis, voice consent, performer terms and output restrictions before production use.

Records support investigation and attribution; they do not guarantee that every output is free of competing rights. Users remain responsible for material they introduce and for release-specific clearance.
