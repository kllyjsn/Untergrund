# Club design notes

The Club should feel like the warehouse version of UNTERGRUND: dark room, hard light, machine characters, and music made inside the instrument.

## Current direction

- Avatars are chunky CSS 3D machines (head, torso, limbs) instead of flat figures. Colour, head shape and gear stay the user's identity.
- Motion follows the set: the bounce and nod use the current BPM, the arms pump on kicks, and chest LEDs flash on accents.
- The DJ is the focal point. The crowd stands on a three-row perspective floor, and anonymous silhouettes fill quiet rooms without pretending to be listeners.

## Future improvements

1. **Character depth:** more silhouettes (tall, wide, small), unlockable gear earned by DJ time, and idle personalities per avatar.
2. **Reactions in space:** HYPE, DEEP and ACID should burst from the avatar who sent them, not the centre of the stage.
3. **Crowd intelligence:** real listeners move closer to the booth as they stay longer or react more; the energy of the set drives crowd density.
4. **Lighting from audio:** drive the beams and floor grid from the analyser (kick, hats, acid filter), not only from step events.
5. **DJ booth as instrument:** show the live pattern, filter sweeps and the next scene on the booth so listeners can see the set being performed.
6. **Room identity:** per-room themes (bunker, rooftop, festival main stage) generated from the host artist's DNA.
7. **Performance budget:** cap the crowd on low-end phones, and offer a WebGL renderer once rooms regularly exceed about 30 people.
8. **Accessibility:** a reduced-motion mode (already respected) plus a listener list for screen readers.
